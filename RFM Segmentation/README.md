# RFM Customer Segmentation

## Introduction

RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis is a customer segmentation methodology used to identify customer value and engagement patterns.

The technique helps organizations prioritize customers based on purchasing behavior and supports data-driven marketing and retention strategies.

---

## RFM Components

### Recency

Measures the number of days since the customer's most recent purchase.

Business Interpretation:

- Lower Recency = More Active Customer
- Higher Recency = Less Active Customer

---

### Frequency

Measures the total number of purchases made by a customer.

Business Interpretation:

- Higher Frequency = More Loyal Customer
- Lower Frequency = Less Engaged Customer

---

### Monetary

Measures the total revenue generated by a customer.

Business Interpretation:

- Higher Monetary Value = Higher Customer Value
- Lower Monetary Value = Lower Revenue Contribution

---

## Business Benefits

RFM analysis enables organizations to:

- Increase customer retention
- Improve marketing effectiveness
- Personalize customer engagement
- Identify churn risks
- Improve customer lifetime value

---

## Retail Analytics Platform Implementation

The Retail Analytics Platform implements RFM analysis using Python-based data processing and customer scoring techniques.

The output generates customer segments and insights that support business decision-making and marketing optimization strategies.

---

The method evaluates customers based on:

Metric	Definition: 


Recency (R)	    How recently a customer made a purchase
Frequency (F)	How often a customer makes purchases
Monetary (M)	How much money the customer spends

By combining these three metrics, customers can be grouped into meaningful business segments that support targeted marketing, customer retention, and revenue optimization strategies.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of RFM Analysis is to:

Identify high-value customers
Recognize loyal customers
Detect customers at risk of churn
Optimize marketing campaigns
Improve customer retention
Increase customer lifetime value (CLV)

---

## Customer Segments

Typical customer segments include:

Segment	Description
Champions	Recent, frequent and high-spending customers
Loyal Customers	Repeat customers with strong engagement
Potential Loyalists	Customers showing growing engagement
Recent Customers	New customers with recent purchases
At Risk	Previously active customers showing reduced engagement
Lost Customers	Customers likely to have churned
