# RFM Customer Segmentation

## Introduction

RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis is a customer segmentation methodology used to identify customer value and engagement patterns.

The technique helps organizations prioritize customers based on purchasing behavior and supports data-driven marketing and retention strategies.

---

## RFM Components

### Recency

Measures the number of days since the customer's most recent purchase.

Business Interpretation:

- Lower Recency = More Active Customer
- Higher Recency = Less Active Customer

---

### Frequency

Measures the total number of purchases made by a customer.

Business Interpretation:

- Higher Frequency = More Loyal Customer
- Lower Frequency = Less Engaged Customer

---

### Monetary

Measures the total revenue generated by a customer.

Business Interpretation:

- Higher Monetary Value = Higher Customer Value
- Lower Monetary Value = Lower Revenue Contribution

---

## Business Benefits

RFM analysis enables organizations to:

- Increase customer retention
- Improve marketing effectiveness
- Personalize customer engagement
- Identify churn risks
- Improve customer lifetime value
